# About AgriSense

## What AgriSense does
AgriSense helps farmers choose crops, understand weather, and prevent crop losses. It works by voice in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and Marathi, and lets you enter soil readings even without internet; data syncs automatically when you are back online.

## How the risk score works
The risk score goes from 0 (safe) to 100 (very risky). It compares your crop's ideal conditions with your soil readings (pH, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, moisture, temperature) and the 16-day weather forecast until your planned harvest: heat or cold stress, heavy rain, dry spells, humid weather that spreads disease, and whether the harvest date matches crop maturity. Scores below 30 are low risk, 30-60 moderate, above 60 high.

## How to take a soil reading
Use a soil test kit, IoT soil sensor, or your Soil Health Card from the government lab. Enter nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium values, soil moisture percent, soil temperature and pH. Test every season before sowing.
